# Design System — Brain Cache

## Product Context

- **What this is:** A private, local-first place to catch thoughts before they disappear.
- **Who it is for:** One person using their own Mac and iPhone.
- **Product posture:** Capture must be faster than organization. The interface stays quiet until summoned.
- **Memorable thing:** The fastest private place to save anything in your head.

## Aesthetic Direction

- **Direction:** Quiet terminal companion.
- **Decoration:** Minimal and intentional. Fine borders, a faint grid, and one creature provide character.
- **Mood:** Warm-black by default, with an optional warm-paper light appearance; keyboard-native software that feels personal without becoming cute or noisy.
- **Design risk:** A single monospace voice carries the entire interface.

## Typography

- **Primary:** Fragment Mono for display, body, controls, metadata, and capture.
- **Fallback:** Apple system monospace until the licensed Fragment Mono font file is bundled.
- **Font size:** Settings → Appearance offers Default (100%), Large (115%), and Extra large (130%). Scale typography throughout the library and capture while preserving the font family and hierarchy. Persist locally before applying, synchronize open windows, and restore on launch without blocking capture. Reflow controls and resize capture while preserving drafts and selection.
- **Long-form text:** Opened notes use a minimum 14 pt with relaxed line spacing. Do not introduce a second body family just to increase readability.
- **Dashboard cards:** Use 17 px body text with 1.7 line spacing across plain text, checklists, and rich documents. Card headings use 26, 22, and 18 px. This slightly denser reading scale is specific to dashboard cards.
- **Labels:** 8–10 pt, medium or semibold, uppercase, with 0.8–1.3 pt tracking.
- **Display:** Tight tracking and compact line-height; amber may emphasize one phrase.

## Color

- **Ink:** `#070706` — app background.
- **Surface:** `#11110F` — navigation and focused detail surfaces.
- **Raised:** `#181816` — cards and capture panel.
- **Hover:** `#201F1C` — interactive hover state.
- **Border:** `#2C2B27` — default dividers and outlines.
- **Strong border:** `#47443D` — focused floating surfaces.
- **Paper:** `#F3F0E7` — primary text and inverted actions.
- **Muted:** `#918D83` — secondary reading text.
- **Subtle:** `#67645D` — metadata.
- **Signal:** `#F2B84B` — capture, life, and attention only.
- **Saved:** `#70C997` — successful local persistence.
- **Error:** `#E56E68` — failed actions that need attention.
- **Tag palette:** Graphite `#7B7972`, Clay `#D99764`, Moss `#9CBF6B`, Sky `#71B7D7`, Plum `#AA91D2`, and Rose `#CE879F`. Tag color is optional metadata, not a status.
- **Tag color treatment:** Use the richer assigned hue as a small dot, a clearly colored border, and a light tint (10–14% at rest, up to 18% when active). Mix the label hue with 25% Paper to keep text readable against the tint. Reserve Signal, Saved, and Error for their semantic meanings rather than ordinary tags.
- **Changing tag colors:** Keep a visible Tag colors control beside the dashboard tag filters, even when the sidebar is collapsed. Its compact picker selects any existing tag, offers the six named palette colors and Remove color, and explains that the choice applies everywhere the tag is used. Save immediately, keep the previous hue until persistence succeeds, and show a retryable error on failure. Escape, Close, or moving outside dismisses the picker. Card and capture tag dots retain their color controls and descriptive hover labels. Right-clicking any part of an attached tag chip in Focus Lens, Canvas Drill-In, or capture opens that tag's existing color picker without removing the tag or opening the system context menu.

### Light Appearance

- **Selection:** Settings → Appearance → Light mode. Off retains the original dark palette; dark remains the default. Persist locally before applying, synchronize the library and capture windows, and restore the choice on launch. Switching appearance preserves drafts, focus, selection, and scrolling. Preference loading never blocks capture.
- **Surfaces:** Ink/background `#F3F0E7`, Surface `#EBE7DC`, Raised `#FFFDF7`, Hover `#E5E0D4`, Border `#D2CDC0`, Strong border `#A8A191`.
- **Text:** Paper/primary ink `#27251F`, Muted `#655F54`, Subtle `#6A6358`.
- **Semantic colors:** Signal `#825507`, Saved `#246E45`, Error `#A63832`. Keep the same meanings with stronger contrast on paper surfaces.
- **Tags:** Use deeper versions of the same named hues: Graphite `#626058`, Clay `#8C542E`, Moss `#526E32`, Sky `#306981`, Plum `#725293`, Rose `#914D66`. Saved tag assignments stay the same across appearances.
- **Treatment:** Preserve typography, density, layout, and motion. Adapt grids, shadows, overlays, form controls, and native window chrome. Capture's surrounding window stays transparent. Blob retains its warm-dark body and existing signal colors.

## Spacing and Shape

- **Base unit:** 4 pt.
- **Density:** Compact and comfortable; optimize for keyboard-driven repetition.
- **Radii:** 7–8 pt controls, 10 pt cards, 14 pt floating capture panel.
- **Borders:** One point. Avoid drop shadows except on floating system surfaces.

## Mac Layout

- **App switching:** Brain Cache is a regular desktop app in the Dock and `⌘ Tab`. Clicking its Dock icon opens or restores the library, including after closing or minimizing its window. Launch at login starts with windows hidden. Menu-bar access and `⌥ Space` capture remain available.
- **Settings:** Open Settings from the sidebar footer, with no dedicated hotkey. When the sidebar is collapsed, keep a Settings gear in the header. Use a compact, scrollable modal with short labels and no explanatory paragraphs or idle footer. Appearance contains light mode and font size; General contains launch at login and sidebar visibility; Capture contains the shortcut button. Show the fixed `⌥ Space` capture shortcut as a reference. Save changes immediately, keep the previous value until persistence succeeds, and show loading or retryable errors per preference. Keep the panel open during a write. Escape, the backdrop, or the X closes an idle panel; trap focus while open and return it to the Settings control. Preserve library search, filters, selection, and scroll. Use neutral switches and the existing surface, type, and border tokens.
- **Library:** Use compact left navigation and the flexible thought-card canvas. A borderless sidebar icon in the top-left header collapses or expands the entire sidebar, including its bottom controls, and remembers the preference locally. Keep the toggle reachable when collapsed and let both the grid and Canvas Drill-In use the released width. Preserve search, filters, card selection, and edits while toggling. Disable the toggle while the modal Focus Lens is open. Do not reserve a permanent right-side inspector. Grid cards have an always-visible Complete button in Saved green and a borderless red trash icon with an accessible Delete name and hover label. Delete moves a note to Trash; Undo and Restore keep it recoverable. Restore keeps its neutral treatment.
- **Card detail:** Selecting a card opens a card-shaped **Focus Lens** above the dimmed grid. It shows the full thought, tags, metadata, Complete, Delete, and close actions, plus an obvious Expand control. Expand uses an icon of two diagonal outward arrows, and Close uses an X; both are borderless icon buttons with accessible names and hover labels. Complete uses Saved green. Delete uses the same borderless red trash icon as the grid, including on hover and keyboard focus. Restore keeps the neutral treatment. Expand turns the same card into **Canvas Drill-In** while leaving navigation in place. Close or Back restores the exact prior grid position, selected card, and keyboard focus.
- **Card sizing:** Use a staggered grid of consistent-width cards whose heights follow their full content. Preserve paragraph breaks and show all checklist items with wrapped text. Do not stretch neighboring cards to equal heights, impose a minimum card height, or truncate the body. Keep document and keyboard order consistent as the grid responds to window resizing, edits, and image loading.
- **Card outlines:** Match the outline to the first assigned colored tag shown on the card, skipping tags with no assigned color. Cards without a colored tag retain their neutral border. Pinned cards always use Signal yellow, overriding tag colors. Keep this precedence on hover, selection, keyboard focus, Focus Lens, and Canvas Drill-In. Apply tag and pin changes after local persistence succeeds; unpinning restores the current tag color.
- **Note completion:** Complete moves a note from All thoughts and Today into Completed after local persistence succeeds. Reopen returns it to active notes. Completing a checklist checks its remaining items and preserves earlier completion dates; unchecking or adding unfinished work reopens it. Completion cancels all note and item reminders. Completed notes remain readable, editable, searchable, and recoverable after deletion.
- **Pinned cards:** Keep a small, always-visible pin icon in each card's metadata row, with an accessible Pin/Unpin name and pressed state. Pinned cards use a quiet amber outline, amber pin, and a small “Pinned” label to mark the user's chosen priorities. Save pin changes locally before reordering. Show pinned cards before other matching cards, keeping the existing newest-first order within each group. Pins respect search, tags, Today, Completed, and Trash; unpinning restores ordinary date order. Pin failures keep the prior state and offer retry on the same card.
- **Rich text editing:** Use the approved **Familiar editor** direction (card-editor-20260910, variant B) in Focus Lens and Canvas Drill-In. A compact header keeps Pin, Complete, red Delete, Expand, and Close reachable. Attached tags and a compact reminder pill share the row below. **Note** and **Details** tabs separate writing from organization; keyboard arrow keys switch tabs. Keep the same editor instance mounted across tabs so selection, undo history, pending file imports, and autosave drafts survive. Details groups tags, the full reminder controls, and capture/source/state metadata. Both tabs retain the same Files list and save/progress row. The Note tab uses one continuous, borderless document surface with a sticky formatting ribbon grouped into **Text**, **Lists**, **Insert**, and **History**: normal text and three heading sizes, bold, italic, underline, bullets, numbered lists, checklists, image insertion, undo, and redo. Keep Fragment Mono and the existing colors. Paragraphs and lists can coexist. Enter adds a list row; Enter on an empty row returns to a paragraph. Checkboxes sit beside flowing text without input boxes or row action buttons. Selecting an unfinished task exposes its item reminder below the document. Preserve stable task IDs, completion history, image order, and formatting through autosave and reopening.
- **Checklist cards:** A thought card shows its full interactive checklist. Items can be checked directly on the card with clear keyboard focus and completed states that remain readable. Full editing belongs in Focus Lens or Canvas Drill-In.
- **Note reminders:** Every active note, including a checklist, offers a compact bell / Remind me pill in the tag row in Focus Lens and Canvas Drill-In. A scheduled pill displays the date and time with visible overdue or failure status. Activating it opens an anchored, viewport-contained date/time popover with a visible time zone, defaulting to the next whole hour. Details also offers the full inline reminder editor. Keep a pending reminder operation visible until it resolves; Escape or outside click dismisses an idle popover and preserves a failed draft for retry. Keep current status, Edit, Clear, notification settings recovery, and retry visible in context. Show the scheduled time quietly on its grid card; use amber for overdue reminders and Error for delivery failures. Save the reminder locally before scheduling through macOS. Opening its notification returns to that note; completing or deleting cancels its reminders. Capture remains free of reminder controls and permission prompts.
- **Task activity:** Keep activity out of the regular dashboard. Use the approved **Garden strip** direction (activity-simple-20260917, variant B) for the sidebar Activity destination: a compact weekly total with Today and All time counts, a small row of growing plants, and the three most recent saved checklist completions today with their local times. Keep the layout borderless, with fine dividers rather than nested cards. A More activity disclosure expands the rest of today's completions, the existing counts, streak, weekly bar chart, and next milestone; keep it closed initially. Missing text for a removed completed item reads “Completed task”; never invent completion dates. Keep navigation, sidebar collapse, New thought, and Back to the prior library view available. Focus the page heading on entry; Back restores search, tags, selection, scroll, and a visible Activity entry point. `⌘K` returns to library search. The Activity button beside New thought retains its quick summary modal, including when the sidebar is collapsed. That modal traps focus, scrolls within the window, and closes with its icon-only X, Escape, or backdrop; closing restores its opener. Counts and recent completions span all checklists, including archived cards, regardless of search or filter. Use Saved green for persisted progress. A brief, nonblocking completion or milestone message follows successful writes; initial loading, editing, and failures stay quiet. Keep motion within 160 ms and honor reduced motion. Earlier checked items count toward the total without invented dates. Unchecking reverses a completion; removing a completed item or archiving its card preserves the record.
- **Activity garden:** Use five small line-drawn plants along a single soil line, with a short growth caption and total finishes below. At zero, show seeds; completions at 1, 5, and 10 grow one, two, and three plants; 25 grows all five with varied heights; 50 adds buds; 100 brings full bloom. The flowering garden remains after 100 while activity totals continue increasing. Keep the garden compact, without a separate card, stage list, large scene, or progress bar. Missed days never wilt plants or reduce growth; unchecking a task reverses its contribution. Existing and undated completions count immediately. Growth uses existing local records, requires no account or network, and never gates capture. Use Saved green for plants, restrained Signal for flowers, and existing neutral colors for the soil line. No watering chores, hunger, loss timers, unsolicited pet speech, or continuous animation.
- **Tags and progress:** Do not repeat the first line as a generated card or detail title. Place tag chips above card content and keep the tag editor in the top of Focus Lens and Canvas Drill-In, with a compact add-tag field even when no tags are attached. Show available-tag suggestions only when the field is activated, rather than reserving a separate always-visible row. Checklist cards show a compact neutral progress bar and completed/total count beside the date; checklist detail shows the same indicator below the writing area. Count the whole checklist, including nonempty new rows in the editor. Progress follows checkbox and item edits; ordinary text and bullet lists have no completion bar.
- **Attachments:** Offer a quiet paperclip / Add files control in both Pet Dock capture and card detail. Accept file selection, file drops, and pasted images. In note detail, the Files section displays only non-image attachments as compact filename/size rows with open and remove actions. Images selected with Add files are inserted into the document; image previews never appear in Files. Capture keeps its existing attachment previews. Use existing surface and border tokens. Capture saves the thought and attached files together; existing-card attachment edits save immediately. Keep the native file picker above the capture panel and keep capture open while picking. Attachment rows expand the same Pet Dock surface and scroll when constrained.
- **Inline screenshots:** Pasting images into a text thought in Pet Dock inserts them at the cursor between the surrounding text, then leaves typing focus after the image. Keep that order through capture, reopening, card display, Focus Lens, and Canvas Drill-In edits. Use a single continuous document surface with text and uncropped image blocks; image removal and opening have compact accessible controls. Reserve the insertion boundary while reading files so later typing or another paste cannot move the image. Retain selected text if importing fails and keep Retry available. Checklist capture retains its separate file shelf; changing an image document into a checklist must not discard its layout.
- **Card images:** Display document images in their saved text/image order at the card's full content width, preserving their aspect ratio without cropping. Legacy attachments and files added with Add files remain below the document. Load image bytes as cards approach the viewport. Other attachments show a compact filename and size. Opening the card exposes attachment editing and file actions.
- **Card autosave:** Text and checklist edits save locally as they change in both Focus Lens and Canvas Drill-In, including text in a new checklist row. Show a quiet saving/saved status without a Save changes control. Keep typing and the caret uninterrupted, finish pending writes before Close, Back, Complete, or Delete, and retain recovery drafts with a Retry action if persistence fails.
- **Capture:** Use the approved **Pet Dock** direction: a compact floating panel near the upper-left of the active screen, inset from the usable screen edges. Its initial content frame targets roughly `455 × 108` logical pixels, with a roughly 38 pt pet badge docked slightly over the left edge, a single-line thought field, a compact Return-key save affordance, and slim tag and file invitations beneath the field. The same rounded surface and native window expand downward as text wraps or an optional control shelf opens. Transparent window chrome surrounds the surface—never a second rectangular backdrop.
- **Shortcut help:** Keep the Pet Dock resting state minimal. A small borderless `⌘` glyph may sit near the save affordance and remain quiet until hover or keyboard focus. Activating it expands the same rounded surface downward into the shortcut drawer rather than opening a detached box. Holding the physical `⌘` key previews the drawer for as long as the key is held, without moving typing focus or the caret; releasing it or leaving the capture window dismisses only that temporary preview. A drawer explicitly opened with the glyph or `⌘?` stays open until dismissed. The glyph may be hidden without disabling shortcuts or the hold preview; **View → Show Shortcut Button** restores it.
- **Capture tags:** Show **Add tags** with `⌘T` and the quiet hint “Find it faster later” from the initial `⌥ Space` opening. Keep typing focus in the thought field until tagging is activated. Attached tags remain visible as colored chips beneath the field after the tag editor closes, with an Edit control and `⌘T` to reopen tagging. The invitation returns whenever no tags are attached; tagging remains optional.
- **Empty state:** The pet and the `⌥ Space` instruction replace placeholder content.
- **Search and tag rail:** Keep search always visible above the thought grid, with “Search thoughts or #tags” as its invitation. Ordinary searches match thought text, checklist text, or tag names, including partial names and regardless of case. A `#tag` term searches assigned tags as the user types; combine it with ordinary text to narrow results. Multiple hashtag terms must all match. Typing `#` also suggests existing tags; choosing a suggestion creates the exact structured tag filter. When tags exist, place one slim, single-line rail directly below search with **All**, up to eight recently used tags, and **More…** for the searchable picker. Use one active rail tag at a time, keep tag text alongside its restrained color treatment, and scroll horizontally rather than wrapping on narrow windows. Search and the rail filter both respect All thoughts, Today, Completed, and Trash.

## Motion

- **Approach:** Minimal and functional.
- **Duration:** 120–180 ms for focus, save, and pet reactions.
- **Rule:** Animation must never delay the first keystroke or local save confirmation.

## Product Rules

- Save locally before any synchronization begins.
- Tags are optional and never delay or gate saving a thought.
- Checklist mode is optional; ordinary thought capture keeps the same number of required actions.
- Shortcut help is optional and must never delay typing, local persistence, or saving. Hiding shortcut hints never disables their commands.
- Reminders are opt-in per note or checklist item. Ask for notification permission only when a person schedules one, never during launch or ordinary capture.
- The pet does not speak unless the user asks it a question.
- Amber means the app noticed something; it is not decorative fill.
- The iPhone is a capture door first and a library second.
